# About E\-SPORT BUSINESS CONFERENCE
E\-sport Business Conference is an event designed for everyone fascinated by the world of gaming—from dedicated fans to those taking their very first steps in the industry.\
Our mission is to build bridges between enthusiasts and the professional world of e-sports.
It is organised during winter by Students\' Science Club "Zarządzanie w Sporcie"[^1] at SGH Warsaw School of Economics
